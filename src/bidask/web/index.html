Each scan asks two questions of every ticker and remembers nothing between scans. First, where its price sits against the reference the current session supplies — the open or yesterday's close during the session, yesterday's close before the bell, the 16:00 print after it, the price 24 hours ago on crypto. Second, whether its Relative Volume at Time clears the floor for that session state: this ticker's volume since 04:00 ET for equities or 00:00 UTC for crypto over the mean volume it had traded by the same point of day across recent sessions. A price move admits nothing on its own. Each chip shows that volume ratio and the move against the reference named beside it. A ticker can legitimately appear in both columns — above today's open and below yesterday's close is one stock being accumulated against one reference and distributed against another, and the labels say which is which.
